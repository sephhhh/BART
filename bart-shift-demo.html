Generate a day of shifts stand-in for the routing script

Generating replaces all shifts, claims and submitted activity. The optimizer builds each shift out of round trips from its start station (19th St, West Oakland, 12th St or MacArthur R). At each step it tries every line, direction and turnaround station and keeps the trip with the best score per minute. The score is estimated riders reached P × a bonus for new lines and zones, reduced for crowded peak trains (peak windows R, crowding factor P). Trips that overlap another shift are never chosen A. It keeps the rules: 15 min admin before boarding R, a 5-min buffer at every connection R, two 15-min breaks and one 45-min meal (+5 min each to reach the break room) R, and ending where the shift started R.
